# SAT Ascent — free adaptive practice for the Digital SAT

**Start practicing:** <https://svesgp.github.io/SAT/>

SAT Ascent is a free practice site built for the Digital SAT. No ads, no fees, no passwords, nothing to install — open it in any browser (phone, tablet or laptop), sign in, and start.

## Getting started

1. **Open the site and sign in.** Use Google, GitHub, or a quick on-device email account — no password is ever created or asked for.
2. **Take the short diagnostic.** About 30 minutes; it finds your starting level and calibrates your tests.
3. **Practice your way.** Start a quick test, drill a topic you got wrong, work through a full module-adaptive test, or just ask the AI tutor.

## What's inside

- **Module-adaptive tests** — just like test day: how you do on Module 1 decides a harder or easier Module 2. Quick (15-minute), standard and full-length tests, all scored out of 1600.
- **Top-band question bank** — every question is tagged Core / Advanced / Elite, and tests weight the Advanced and Elite bands that separate 1400 from 1550+.
- **Topic lessons** — a focused primer for every SAT domain (the four Reading & Writing domains and the four Math domains), with strategies, worked examples and the traps students actually fall into. Finish with an instant topic drill.
- **Honest stats** — the dashboard counts only questions you actually answer (skipping records nothing), tracks accuracy per domain and difficulty band, and shows exactly where to focus next.
- **Personalized tips** — after each test you get a ranked, concrete plan: the two domains to fix, the exact techniques, and a 7-day drill schedule.
- **Wrong-answer review** — every question you miss is kept and can be rebuilt into review tests until you've got it.
- **Free AI tutor** 🤖 — a button on every question and in the corner of every screen. Ask for a hint (it won't spoil the answer before you've tried), a step-by-step explanation after you answer, or open study chat in your own words. Free, no signup, no API key.
- **Updates feed** — official College Board news and SAT community updates, tracked automatically twice a day, so you never miss a testing change.

## Your data stays yours

- Your progress lives **only in your browser**, sealed inside an encrypted record that only this app can read. There are no servers holding your data.
- **Sign out** any time — your record is parked safely under your account, and everything continues exactly where you left off when you sign back in. Different students can share one device without overwriting each other.
- **Export your progress** as a file from Settings whenever you want a copy.
- The AI tutor receives only the text you send it (or the question you attach) — never your record, email or progress.

## Question quality

Every built-in question is original — written in the style and difficulty bands of the official Digital SAT, tagged by difficulty, and shipped with a full explanation of the answer *and* the trap. The bank also folds in openly licensed question banks (verified licenses only) so the pool keeps growing.
